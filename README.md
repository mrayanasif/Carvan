CARVAN is a responsive online car showroom website designed to help users explore car brands, browse car models, view vehicle images and specifications, and connect with prospective customers. The project is based on the CARVAN eProject brief, which describes an online car market providing car information, pictures, and details for buyers. fileciteturn2file0L81-L115

## Features

- **Home page** with visual sections and CARVAN branding.
- **Navigation menu** for About Us, Brands, Range of Cars, PreOwned Cars, and Contact Us.
- **Car brands and models** with brand information and model browsing.
- **Car gallery** with vehicle images and presentation cards.
- **Range of cars** organized into categories such as small cars, sedans, vans, and jeeps.
- **About Us** section for company information and events.
- **Contact Us** page/section with company contact information and an email link where configured.
- **Responsive layout** with a mobile navigation menu.

> The feature list above reflects the project brief and the website context. Exact functionality depends on the pages and assets included in the repository.

## Responsive Design

The layout includes responsive styling for smaller screens. The gallery page's mobile layout and navigation were adjusted during development to resolve a width issue caused by the navbar positioning.

## Project Requirements from the SRS

The supplied CARVAN project brief specifies these main requirements:

1. Create a home page with suitable sections, graphics, and a logo.
2. Provide navigation for About Us, Brands, Range of Cars, PreOwned Cars, and Contact Us.
3. Show information about car brands/companies and provide model submenus.
4. Present vehicle categories such as small cars, sedans, vans, and jeeps.
5. Include pre-owned car information.
6. Provide company information and past/upcoming events in About Us.
7. Provide a Contact Us page with company address and a clickable email link. fileciteturn2file0L93-L115
